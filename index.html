LLM Pipeline Explorer

An interactive educational visualizer showing how Large Language Models turn raw words into subword tokens and navigate 3D semantic embedding spaces.

Section 1

Subword Tokenizer: Vocabulary & Decomposition

LLMs cannot process full words infinitely; they build a vocabulary of the most frequent words and break rarer words down into subwords or single letters.

How Subword Tokenization Works: When a word isn't in the top-N vocabulary, the tokenizer greedily searches for the longest piece present in the vocabulary. If no subword matches, it splits it into single individual characters.
Section 2

Semantic Embeddings in 3D Space

Words and sentences are converted into coordinates (vectors). Here, each word is mapped to 3 conceptual axes: X = Animal, Y = Technology, and Z = Finance. Sentence vectors are calculated as the average of their word vectors.
